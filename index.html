Our Story

Where Every Cup Has a History

BrewHaven was founded in Edinburgh in 2012 by two friends who had spent years travelling the coffee-growing regions of Ethiopia, Colombia, and Indonesia. Frustrated by the gap between café quality and what was available to home brewers, they set out to build a shop that offered the same beans, machines, and knowledge used by world-class baristas — accessible to everyone.

Every SKU in our catalogue comes with a detailed origin story and history, because we believe understanding where your coffee comes from makes every sip more meaningful. From 9th-century Ethiopian highlands to the cutting-edge pressure profiling technology of today's espresso machines — coffee has a rich story, and we want to share it with you.

We source directly from small-holder farmers and certified cooperatives, paying above Fairtrade prices, and we roast in small batches at our Edinburgh roastery to ensure peak freshness in every bag.
